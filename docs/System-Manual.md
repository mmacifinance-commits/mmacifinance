## 1. Overview

This Accounting Information System is used to manage budget fund utilization and tracking. It records income, creates budget appropriations, tracks expenses and disbursements, and shows summaries through dashboards and reports.

The system is designed so that all records are connected from the source of funds down to the final posted transaction.

## 2. Purpose of the System

The system helps users:

- record income
- create and manage budgets
- assign appropriations
- link budget items to account titles and responsibility centers
- record expenses
- prepare disbursements
- approve and post transactions
- monitor balances, utilization, and reports

## 3. Main System Flow

The general flow of the system is:

1. Income is recorded.
2. Appropriation is created from the income source.
3. An annual budget is created.
4. Budget categories, responsibility centers, and account titles are assigned.
5. Monthly budget items are prepared.
6. Expenses are recorded.
7. Disbursements are created.
8. Disbursements are approved or returned.
9. Posted transactions reduce the available balance.
10. Dashboard and revenue summaries update automatically.

## 4. Step-by-Step Explanation

### 4.1 Recording Income

Income is the money that comes into the system.

Examples:

- tuition
- laboratory fees
- trust fund income
- auxiliary income

This is the source of the budget.

### 4.2 Creating Appropriation

Appropriation is the amount set aside from income for spending.

This means:

- income is the total source amount
- appropriation is the part assigned for use

The system uses appropriation as the controlled budget amount.

### 4.3 Creating the Annual Budget

The annual budget is the master budget for the fiscal year.

It stores:

- fiscal year
- reference number
- coverage or semester
- monthly budget structure

### 4.4 Setting Budget Categories

Budget categories are used to group related budget items.

Examples:

- Auxiliary Fund
- Trust Fund
- Tuition and Other Fees
- Laboratory Fees
- Miscellaneous Income

Categories help users organize the budget into major fund types.

### 4.5 Assigning Responsibility Centers

A responsibility center is the office or unit responsible for the budget item.

Examples:

- Administration
- Marine Engineering
- Information System
- Senior High School

This shows which unit owns or uses the budget.

### 4.6 Creating Account Titles

Account titles are the detailed budget lines under a category and responsibility center.

Examples:

- Insurance Expense
- Graduation Expense
- Medical-Dental Fund
- Laboratory Supplies
- Energy Fee
- Testing Fee

An account title tells the system:

- what the budget line is for
- which category it belongs to
- which responsibility center it belongs to
- how it should be tracked

### 4.7 Creating Monthly Budget Items

The annual budget is broken into monthly budget items.

Each budget item includes:

- month
- category
- responsibility center
- account title
- appropriation amount

This is how the system records the budget in detail.

### 4.8 Recording Expenses

An expense is recorded when money is used.

The expense should be linked to:

- category
- responsibility center
- account title
- date
- amount

This allows the system to know exactly where the money was spent.

### 4.9 Creating Disbursements

A disbursement is the payment or release record for an expense.

It usually includes:

- disbursement reference number
- expense reference
- payee
- amount
- payment method
- workflow status

Disbursements are used to track the movement of money.

### 4.10 Approval and Posting

Disbursements go through approval before they are finalized.

Possible statuses include:

- prepared
- submitted
- for approval
- approved
- posted
- returned
- rejected

When a transaction is posted, it becomes official and updates the balance.

### 4.11 Dashboard Updates

The dashboard shows the summary of the system.

It may display:

- annual budget
- expenditures
- remaining balance
- utilization rate
- recent transactions
- chart summaries

The dashboard changes based on filters such as:

- year
- month
- responsibility center
- category
- account title
- date range

### 4.12 Revenue Updates

The revenue page shows the relationship between:

- income
- appropriation
- expenses
- remaining appropriation

This page helps users understand how the budget source was used.

## 5. User Roles and Responsibilities

### 5.1 Head of Finance / Super Admin

The Head of Finance is the highest role in the system.

Responsibilities:

- add and manage income
- review budgets
- approve disbursements
- post disbursements
- reject disbursements
- return disbursements for correction
- view all reports and dashboards
- monitor the full accounting flow

### 5.2 Budget Officer

Responsibilities:

- manage annual budgets
- manage budget categories
- manage account titles
- create and update budget items
- maintain responsibility center assignments

### 5.3 Disbursement Officer

Responsibilities:

- prepare expense records
- prepare disbursement records
- submit records for approval
- monitor workflow status

### 5.4 Cashier

Responsibilities:

- assist in preparing disbursements
- process release-related tasks
- submit disbursement entries into the workflow

The Cashier cannot perform final approval.

### 5.5 Auditor

Responsibilities:

- review transaction records
- inspect audit trails
- verify totals and posting history
- confirm that the workflow matches the stored records

## 6. Data Consistency Rules

To keep the system accurate:

- income should be recorded first
- appropriation should come from income
- budget items should use the correct category, responsibility center, and account title
- expenses should be linked to the correct budget item
- disbursements should be tied to expenses
- approvals should follow the user role permissions
- posted records should update totals automatically

## 9. Detailed Per-Role Procedures

### 9.1 Head of Finance / Super Admin

1. Log in to the system.
2. Review the dashboard summary.
3. Open the Income module when income records need to be added or reviewed.
4. Check the Budget module for appropriation and budget structure.
5. Open Disbursements to review pending transactions.
6. Approve, return, reject, or post disbursements as needed.
7. Review Revenue and Reports to confirm the system totals are correct.

### 9.2 Budget Officer

1. Log in to the system.
2. Open the Budget module.
3. Create or update annual budgets.
4. Assign budget categories.
5. Maintain responsibility center assignments.
6. Manage account titles and budget items.
7. Check whether the budget structure matches the expected allocation.

### 9.3 Disbursement Officer

1. Log in to the system.
2. Open Expenditures or Disbursements.
3. Prepare the expense record.
4. Create the disbursement entry.
5. Submit the record for approval.
6. Monitor the workflow status until it is approved or returned.

### 9.4 Cashier

1. Log in to the system.
2. Open Disbursements.
3. Prepare disbursement details.
4. Submit the disbursement for workflow review.
5. Wait for the Head of Finance to approve or post the record.

### 9.5 Auditor

1. Log in to the system.
2. Open the dashboard, reports, and audit trail pages.
3. Review transactions and totals.
4. Confirm that approvals and postings are correct.
5. Check whether the records match the workflow history.

## 10. Common Questions and Troubleshooting

### Why does the system use appropriation instead of only income?

Because income is the source of funds, while appropriation is the amount assigned for use in the budget.

### Why are account titles important?

Account titles identify the specific budget line being used, so the system can track where the money belongs.

### Why is the dashboard not showing the expected data?

Check the filters first:

- selected year
- selected month
- responsibility center
- category
- account title
- date range

If the filters are too narrow, some records may not appear.

### Why can I not approve a disbursement?

Approval is restricted by role. Only the authorized final approver can complete that step.

### What should I do if a record looks wrong?

Review the linked category, responsibility center, account title, and date. If needed, return the record for correction instead of posting it.
